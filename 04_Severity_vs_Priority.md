# Severity vs Priority

## Severity

### Definition
Severity refers to the impact of a defect on the application's functionality.

### Levels
- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

### Decided By
Tester / QA Engineer

---

## Priority

### Definition
Priority refers to how quickly a defect should be fixed.

### Levels
- High
- Medium
- Low

### Decided By
Project Manager / Product Owner / Client

---

# Difference Between Severity and Priority

| Severity | Priority |
|----------|----------|
| Indicates the impact of a defect | Indicates the urgency to fix a defect |
| Decided by the Tester | Decided by the Project Manager or Product Owner |
| Related to system functionality | Related to business needs |
| Can be High even if Priority is Low | Can be High even if Severity is Low |

---

# Examples

## High Severity & High Priority
- Login button is not working.

## High Severity & Low Priority
- Application crashes on an admin page that is rarely used.

## Low Severity & High Priority
- Company logo is incorrect on the home page before product launch.

## Low Severity & Low Priority
- Minor spelling mistake on a help page.
